<h3 align="center">ABOUT ME</h3>
<br/>


<div align="justify">

My journey in technology has grown from curiosity into a strong interest in building systems that are practical, intelligent, and meaningful. I am a Computer Science student with a growing focus on Artificial Intelligence, Data Analysis, and Research, particularly in applying technology to real-world issues.

I am interested in how AI can move beyond simple automation to become a tool for identifying patterns, improving decision-making, supporting people, and solving problems across domains such as education, well-being, accessibility, business, and everyday digital experiences. I enjoy exploring how data can reveal useful insights, how research can shape better solutions, and how software can turn those ideas into something people can actually use.

My work often combines coding, AI workflows, data visualisation, and user-focused design. I like approaching a problem by first understanding the context, studying possible solutions, analysing the available information, and then building something clear, reliable, and purposeful.

I believe good software should not only function correctly, but also make sense to the people who use it. My goal is to keep learning, researching, and building systems that are useful, thoughtful, and capable of creating real impact.
